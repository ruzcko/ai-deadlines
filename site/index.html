Each milestone type is its own feed: subscribe to just what you care about and color them separately. Feeds refresh daily and never include estimated dates.

Dates come from huggingface/ai-deadlines (MIT), with gaps filled and dates cross-checked from PaperRush (Apache-2.0). “✓ 2 sources” means both list the same date; “⚠ Sources differ” means they don’t. Deadlines move and get extended — always confirm on the official call for papers. Spotted a wrong date? Use “Report a wrong date” on any venue.
