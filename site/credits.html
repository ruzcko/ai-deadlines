The venues’ official pages

Our second source. Once a week a script fetches each venue’s important-dates page and has a language model (Google’s Gemini) extract the dates into a fixed format; the result is stored in the repository and shown with an “Official” tag. It fills gaps in the Hugging Face data (host cities, conference dates, editions not listed yet) and is compared with it (“✓ 2 sources” / “⚠ Sources differ”). Nothing is inferred: a date is reported only if the page states it.

Between 10 and 11 October 2026 the site also used data from PaperRush (Apache-2.0) while this was being built. Some of its feature ideas (quick links on a venue, shareable lists, a calendar view, report-a-date links) are implemented independently here; no PaperRush code, data or design assets remain in use.

A note on accuracy

Deadlines move and get extended. Always confirm on the venue's official call for papers. If you spot a wrong date, the “Report a wrong date” link on any venue opens a ready-to-send issue with whichever source listed it.
